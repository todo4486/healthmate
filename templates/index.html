How it works

Three simple steps

  1. Create your free account

    Sign up with your email or Google account. Your data stays private to you.

  2. Build daily habits

    Choose a few small, healthy habits and tick them off each day.

  3. Get guidance when you need it

    Check symptoms, ask the assistant or read the library, with clear advice on when to see a professional.
